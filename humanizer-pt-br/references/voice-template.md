# Voice profile template

Optional. To have the skill rewrite in your voice, copy this file as `references/voice.md` (same folder) and fill it in, in Portuguese or English. The more real examples, the better the result.

## Essence
Three or four sentences about how you sound. Example: "Direto e próximo, falo como quem está dentro da operação. Explico pelo benefício, nunca pela tecnologia."

## Rhythm
Short or long sentences? More spoken or more formal? Do you ask the reader questions? Do you use parentheses?

## Editing marks
The most useful section. Take a text the AI wrote for you, edit it by hand, and compare block by block. Write down what you changed, with the before and after. Examples of what usually shows up: commas where the AI put periods, connectors you add back ("afinal de contas", "por exemplo"), words you always swap ("A propósito" instead of "Aliás"), claims you soften, jargon you explain inside the sentence, exaggerations you cut, punctuation of your own ("Mas..", ":D", "rs"). For each mark, say how often it appears in a text.

## Marks the AI must not imitate
Things that are yours but are not style, such as typos or double spaces. The skill points them out and does not copy them.

## Expressions that are yours
Catchphrases, openings, closings and terms you coined, with what each one means.

## Words you never use
Terms that sound fake coming from you.

## Personality and dose
Asides, jokes, parallels between different worlds, books you recommend. Say how many of each fit in one text before it becomes caricature.

## Stories and facts you allow
True stories, numbers and details the skill may use to anchor a sentence. Anything not listed here or in the original text is never added.

## Registers by channel
How you write a client email, a post, an article, a message to your team.

## Real examples
Paste two or three passages you wrote and consider your best voice.
