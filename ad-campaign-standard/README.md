# Ad Campaign Standard

Paid social campaigns that buy conversations or sales at a cost the business can afford, worked in order: business math, destination, structure, audience and retargeting, angles, creative, tracking, build, first week and iteration. Builds a new campaign with approval gates before production and before publishing, or reviews a running one with ranked findings.

## Why

Most campaigns fail before the first ad runs: a budget that cannot reach the goal, an objective that optimizes for clicks, a page that leaks, copy that reads like a machine wrote it, or no way to know which ad brought the sale. This standard works the chain in order, because each link depends on the one before it.

## The ten blocks

| Block | What it settles |
|---|---|
| 1. Business math | Goal, funnel worked backwards, what the budget buys; a gate on the plan before anything is produced |
| 2. Destination | One action, a page that passes the Landing Page Standard, a price policy, someone who owns the response |
| 3. Structure | Objective equal to the business result, few ad sets with enough data, names that travel into the report |
| 4. Audience and retargeting | A required audience card (persona, location, age, signals, exclusions, each with its reason, checked against who actually converts), broad delivery with signals, converters excluded, retargeting audiences created on day one |
| 5. Angles | One buyer situation and one pain per ad, explained for someone who never heard of the offer |
| 6. Creative and copy | A hook in two seconds without sound, formats that carry considered offers, every word through the humanizer; a gate on a review page |
| 7. Tracking | One conversion event everywhere, dynamic UTMs, the ad name carried into the conversation, a sales log |
| 8. Build | Account readiness, platform AI off, placements per format, previews checked, drafts approved before publishing, and a required plan-to-live check after publishing and after every edit |
| 9. First week | Hands off for seven days, watch only for breakage, every change written in the campaign's history |
| 10. Read and iterate | A dashboard for non-specialists, ranges per ad, one hypothesis a week, scale in steps, retargeting turned on when the audience passes about 1,000 people |
